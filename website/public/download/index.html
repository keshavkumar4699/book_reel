Download

Get Cloro.

Free on Google Play for Android 7.0 and later. Bring your own books, or begin with Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland.

Get it on Google Play
On a computer? Scan with your phone.

What you’ll need

Android 7.0 or laterCloro is for Android phones. It isn’t on iPhone yet.
The natural voiceOptional, a one-time 103 MB download. Then it reads offline.
On-device AIOptional, for 64-bit phones on Android 11 or later: 345 MB to 2 GB.

No account needed to read. Online features (syncing between phones, the Journal feed and following) use one, and can be turned off in Settings.

Your first five minutes

  1. Open Alice, or import a book. In Library, tap Import and pick a DRM-free EPUB or a text PDF.
  2. Swipe up. Each reel is a screen of the book. Tap any word for its meaning; long-press to highlight.
  3. Set a goal. Cards or minutes, daily or weekly. Your streak starts when you meet it.
  4. Try Listen. Tap Listen on the right. For the natural voice, download it in Settings → Read aloud.
