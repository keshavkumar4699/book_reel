Delete your Cloro account

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time. It takes effect straight away and can’t be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Profile and tap ⚙ Settings.
  2. Under You, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Want a copy first? Tap Download my data just above it: one file with everything on your phone and everything our servers keep about you.

What’s deleted

From Cloro’s servers, at once: your profile and @username, who you follow and who follows you, your reading activity and sessions, comments and kudos, synced highlights, saved cards, words and notes, and the share links you made.

Books and notes kept only on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app. Records of AI costs are kept for accounting without anything that identifies you.

No longer have the app?

Email support@cloro.in from the address on your account, or tell us your @username, and we’ll delete the account within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription, because Google Play bills it. Cancel in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.
