Plans and billing

What counts as an AI book?

A book counts once Claude prepares its first chapter for you. Reading it again, or a book someone else already had prepared, doesn’t count again. Books on your phone’s own cards never count.

Is there a free trial?

When a plan has a free trial, Google Play shows it before you subscribe, and the plan list in the app says so. You can cancel during the trial and pay nothing.

How do I subscribe, switch or cancel?

Subscribe or switch in the app: Profile → ⚙ Settings → Plan. Switching credits the time left on your old plan. Cancel in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions; your plan lasts until the end of the time you paid for.

What happens when a plan ends?

You’re back on Free. Your books, notes, highlights and streak stay; new cards are cut on your phone again.

Why are prices different in India?

Plans are priced for each region, and Google Play charges in your currency. In India the price is lower and the monthly AI books are fewer.

I have a new phone. How do I get my plan back?

Use the same Google Play account, then open Settings → Plan → Restore purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy. You can ask for one from your Google Play order history.
