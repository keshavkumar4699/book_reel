Short videos are built to keep you scrolling. Cloro keeps the ease and drops the endless. One page on screen. One swipe to move on. Sessions that end, a goal that’s yours, and only real reading counts.

How it reads

A book, one reel at a time.

01

Swipe up. Keep reading.

Real pages from your own EPUBs and PDFs, cut at natural places, never mid-thought. A book opens at its first chapter, not the copyright page.

02

Tap a word. Know it.

An offline dictionary answers at a tap, and harder words are gently underlined. Keep the ones you want in your word list, for spaced review.

03

Listen, hands-free.

A natural, human-sounding voice reads on your phone: free, offline, unlimited. Reels turn by themselves, and the screen stays on while you listen.

04

Keep what moves you.

Long-press to highlight and drag the handles to fit. Swipe left to save, right to share a quote card. It all lands in your Notebook, ready to export as Markdown.

05

A goal you can keep.

Choose cards or minutes a day. Watch the ring fill, keep your streak with a freeze when life happens, and find every session in your Journal.

Questions, answered.

What can I read in Cloro?

Your own DRM-free EPUBs and text PDFs, and public-domain classics. Cloro comes with Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland to start. It doesn’t open copy-protected files.

Is Cloro free?

Yes. The Free plan cuts cards on your phone, reads aloud with a natural voice offline, and has every scene, goal and streak. Paid plans add Claude’s reading for sharper cards. See plans.

Does it work offline?

Reading, the dictionary, the natural voice and on-device AI all work offline. Syncing between phones, the Journal feed and AI cards on paid plans need a connection.

Is Cloro on iPhone?

Not yet. Cloro is for Android 7.0 and later.

What happens to my data?

Your books stay on your phone. With online features on, we keep what your account and feed need, and you can download it or delete your account at any time. Read the privacy policy.
