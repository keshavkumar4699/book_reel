Features

Everything a book needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

Real pages, one calm reel at a time, with help when a word or a chapter gets hard, and a habit that sticks without nagging.

Reading

Reels made of real pages.

Import your own books and read them one screen at a time. Swipe up for the next reel; Cloro keeps your place to the word.

  • EPUB and text PDF, from your phone’s files
  • Opens at the first chapter, past the title page and copyright
  • Cuts at natural breaks, never in the middle of a thought
  • Chapter cards with the reels and minutes ahead
  • Spotlight reels give the most quotable line a screen of its own
  • Page mode, when you want a whole page at once

Understanding

Help when a page gets hard.

Tap any word for its meaning, offline. Short recaps and quick checks keep a long chapter in your head, and reflect cards ask what you think.

  • An offline dictionary, at a tap
  • Harder words underlined, at the reading level you choose
  • A word list with spaced review, so new words stay
  • Recaps and quick checks: on your phone on the Free plan, by Claude on paid plans
  • Reflect cards, and notes for your own thoughts

Listening

A voice that sounds like a person. Free.

Download the natural voice once and it reads on your phone: no internet, no account, no limit. Reels turn as they’re read, and every one you hear counts.

  • A neural voice that runs on the phone (a one-time 103 MB download)
  • The next reels are made ready while one plays, so it never waits
  • The screen stays on while you listen; turn it off and the book reads on
  • Your music turns down while the voice reads
  • Speeds from 0.8× to 1.3×, or your phone’s own voice
Reading aloud · offline

Notebook

A notebook that fills itself.

Everything you mark while reading lands in one place: highlights, saved cards, words and reflections, newest first.

  • Long-press to highlight, then drag the handles to fit
  • Four highlight colours, and a note on any highlight
  • Swipe left to save a reel; double-tap to like one
  • Liked quotes on your profile
  • Export everything as Markdown
  • The same notebook on each of your phones, with online features on

Habits

A habit, without the guilt.

Pick a goal that fits your day and watch it fill. Sessions end, and a streak freeze covers the day life gets in the way.

  • Daily or weekly goals, in cards or minutes
  • Streak freezes, and one gentle reminder a day
  • Only real reading counts: a reel flicked past too fast moves you on but adds nothing
  • Your Journal: every session, with the book’s progress
  • Profile stats for the last 7 days, the month, the year and all time
  • Trophies for streaks, books and cards

Social

Read with people you know.

Follow friends, see their reading in your Journal and cheer them on. Share a passage or your progress as a card that looks good anywhere.

  • Follow by @username, or swap invite codes to follow each other
  • Kudos and comments on reading sessions
  • A weekly league with the readers you follow, or hide rankings
  • Quote and progress cards, Strava-style, in several looks and sizes
  • Block and report, whenever you need to

Make it yours

Every reel, your way.

Tap the screen and the controls appear: the pictures to read on at the top; the book, chapter and music at the bottom.

  • Eleven painted scenes that move gently, three of them study scenes
  • Your own photos, measured so the text stays readable
  • Your own music, with previous, pause and next
  • 17 reading fonts, or fonts that fit each book
  • Light, dark, or your phone’s setting
  • Nine languages: English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português, Русский

Accessibility

Made for every reader.

Cloro is built to work with the settings you already use.

TalkBack

Every button and reel is labelled, and the controls stay up until you hide them.

Larger text

Screens make room for your phone’s larger text sizes, in every language.

Fonts for every eye

Atkinson Hyperlegible for low vision, and OpenDyslexic.

Left-handed layout

The rail of actions moves to the left side.

Calmer motion

Scenes hold still when your phone asks to remove animations.

Readable on any picture

Text keeps at least 7:1 contrast on every scene and photo.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your books never leave your phone as files. What does go online is yours to switch off.

  • No book file is ever uploaded
  • The voice and on-device AI run on your phone
  • Online features (sync, the feed, AI cards) can be turned off in Settings
  • No ads, and we don’t sell your data
  • Download everything we keep, or delete your account, any time
  • Crash reports never include your books, and can be turned off
