Support

How can we help?

Getting started

How do I add my own books?

Open Library, tap Import and pick a file from your phone. Cloro reads EPUB and PDF files that you have the right to read: your own DRM-free purchases, public-domain books, or books you’re licensed to use.

Why won’t a book open?

Cloro can’t open copy-protected (DRM) books. A PDF made of scanned page images has no text to read, so Cloro can’t turn it into reels either. Try the EPUB edition, or a PDF with selectable text.

What do the gestures do?
  • Swipe up: the next reel.
  • Tap a word: its meaning.
  • Long-press a word: highlight it, then drag the handles.
  • Double-tap: like the reel.
  • Swipe left: save it. Swipe right: share it.
  • Tap empty space: show the pictures, music and tabs for a few seconds.
  • Long-press empty space: theme, contents, page mode and more.
Where does a book start?

At its first chapter or introduction, past the title page and copyright. After that, Cloro keeps your place to the word. Jump anywhere from Contents in the options (long-press empty space).

Reading and listening

How do I listen to a book?

Tap Listen on the right of a reel. For the natural voice, open Profile → ⚙ Settings → Read aloud and download it once (103 MB); it then reads offline with no limit. Without it, your phone’s own voice reads.

Does listening count towards my goal?

Yes. Reels turn by themselves as they’re read, and each one you hear counts. The screen stays on while you listen; if you turn it off, the book keeps reading and still counts.

How do I change the font, text size or theme?

In Settings → Text & theme: 17 reading fonts (or each book’s own), the text size and the reading theme. Light or dark screens are in Settings → Appearance.

How do I change the background or the music?

Tap empty space on a reel. The row of pictures appears at the top: pick one, or tap + to add photos from your phone (long-press one of yours to remove it). The music’s previous, pause and next are at the bottom. More in Settings → Backgrounds and Settings → Music.

Can I read a full page instead of reels?

Yes. Long-press empty space and choose Page mode. Closing it brings you back to the reel you were on.

Goals and streaks

Why didn’t a reel count?

Only real reading counts. A reel flicked past faster than careful reading (500 words a minute, or faster than the pace Cloro has learned for you) moves your place on, but adds nothing to your goal, streak, stats or the feed.

How do I change my goal?

Open Settings → Daily goal, or tap the goal on a reel. Choose cards or minutes, and a daily or weekly goal.

What is a streak freeze?

A freeze covers a day you miss, so your streak carries on. You get one a week on Free, and up to five on paid plans.

Account and friends

Do I need an account?

No. Reading, listening, notes and streaks work on your phone without one. Online features (syncing between phones, the Journal feed, following and AI cards on paid plans) use an account, and you can turn them off in Settings → Online features.

How do I follow a friend?

Open Profile and tap the person icon to find people by @username. Or swap invite codes, and you’ll follow each other.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile, or a comment of theirs, and choose block or report. Blocked readers can’t find, follow or comment on you. Manage them in Settings → Blocked readers.

Plans and billing

How do I subscribe or switch plans?

In Settings → Plan. Google Play shows the price in your currency before you subscribe, and switching credits the time left on your old plan. Compare plans.

How do I cancel?

In the Play Store app: Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Cloro → Cancel. Your plan lasts until the end of the time you paid for.

How do I restore my plan on a new phone?

Use the same Google Play account, then open Settings → Plan → Restore purchases.

Privacy and your data

Are my books uploaded anywhere?

No book file ever leaves your phone. On paid plans, the text of the chapters you read goes to our AI provider only to prepare your cards. Read the privacy policy.

How do I download my data?

Open Settings → You → Download my data. You get one file with everything on your phone and everything Cloro’s servers keep about you.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → You → Delete account. It erases your account and everything tied to it from our servers straight away. No longer have the app? Here’s how.

Can I turn off crash reports?

Yes, in Settings → Send crash reports. They say what went wrong and where in the app, never what you were reading.
