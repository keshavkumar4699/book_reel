About

The ease of a feed.
The depth of a book.

We read less than we’d like, and scroll more than we mean to. Cloro is a bet that the second can help the first.

A feed is easy to begin: one thing on screen, one swipe to move on, no decisions. A book asks more of you before it gives anything back. So Cloro keeps the ease of the feed and puts a book behind it: real pages from the books you choose, one calm reel at a time.

What we don’t borrow is the endless part. Feeds are built to keep you there. Cloro is built for you to finish the book and get on with your day: sessions end, goals are yours to set, and a reel flicked past without reading counts for nothing.

What we hold to

A calm feed

A quiet page with the book’s own type, on a scene that moves slowly. No autoplay noise, no badges begging for a tap.

Sessions that end

A goal you set, a ring that fills and a clear finish, instead of a feed with no bottom.

Only real reading counts

Skimming moves your place on but adds nothing to goals, streaks, stats or leagues. Your numbers mean something.

Help that sticks

Meanings at a tap, short recaps, quick checks and a word list with spaced review, so what you read stays with you.

Yours to own

Your books stay on your phone, online features are optional, and you can download or delete everything at any time.

Paid by readers

No ads, and we don’t sell data. Plans pay for the AI and the servers; reading itself stays free.

How we measure it

Books finished.
Not minutes spent.

We judge Cloro by how many books its readers finish, how much they remember, and whether they’d say it made their week better. If a change makes people spend more time but finish fewer books, it’s the wrong change.
