## The short version

- Your books stay on your phone. We never upload a book file.
- With **Online features** off (Settings → Comfort & privacy), nothing leaves your phone except what Google Play needs for subscriptions.
- With online features on, we keep what's needed for your account, your reading feed and AI help, described below. You can download all of it or delete your account at any time in Settings → You.
- We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

## What stays on your phone

Your books, notes, highlights, saved cards, word list, reading history, mood log, settings and profile photo. The Free plan's on-device AI runs on your phone; nothing it reads is sent anywhere.

## What we keep when online features are on

- **Account:** a random identifier made by the app (or your Google account when you sign in), your display name, @username, pronouns, bio, avatar emoji, up to 3 favourite genres, and the title of the book you're reading. Other readers can see these.
- **Following:** who you follow and who follows you. Follower and following counts are public.
- **Reading activity:** for each card read, the card's position in the book, how many words it had and how long you spent on it. We use this to check streaks and leagues.
- **Reading sessions:** book title and author, minutes, cards, how far into the book you are, your mood if you set one, and one line you saved, only from public-domain books. Readers who follow you see these in their feed.
- **Comments and kudos** you give and receive.
- **Synced notes:** highlights, saved cards, words, reflections and reading positions, so they're the same on your phones.
- **Share links** you create (the quote or progress you shared).
- **Reports and blocks** you make, and reports about you.
- **Crash reports** (unless you turn them off): the error, where in the app it happened, the app version and platform. No book text is sent on purpose.
- **Usage counters** that enforce plan limits (books per month, read-aloud characters, mood suggestions).

## Services that process data for us

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud (our servers, logs) | everything listed above | to run Cloro |
| Google Play | your purchases | subscriptions and billing; we receive the purchase status, never your payment details |
| Anthropic (Claude) | on paid plans, the chapter text of books you read, and a book's title, author, tags and opening pages | to cut cards, write recaps and choose fonts |
| DeepInfra | the text being read aloud when you use the natural voice; your mood log (when, mood, minutes) for mood suggestions | read-aloud and mood suggestions |
| Firebase (Google) | your Google account, when you sign in | sign-in |

These services process data only to provide their service to us. AI providers receive book text only while preparing your cards; we don't use your books to train models.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data:** until you delete the account.
- **Server logs:** about 30 days.
- **AI cost records:** kept for accounting without your identifier after you delete your account.
- **Purchase records:** stay with Google Play under its own terms.

## Your choices and rights

- **Download** everything: Settings → You → Download my data.
- **Delete** your account and everything tied to it: Settings → You → Delete account, or at [your domain]/account/delete. It takes effect straight away.
- **Correct** your profile: Profile → Edit profile.
- **Turn off** online features, crash reports or mood suggestions in Settings at any time.
- **Ask us** anything, or complain, at [contact email]. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Cloro isn't for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) because of its social features.

## Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Keys and passwords are kept in a secret store. Access is limited to what's needed to run Cloro.

## Changes

We'll tell you in the app before a change that affects what we collect or how we use it.
