# Your music

Put the music you want while reading in this folder, then build the app
again. Each book starts one of your tracks at random and plays on through
them; while reading, tap the screen for previous, pause and next.

- **Formats:** MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, WAV or FLAC.
- **Size:** every track is packed into the app, so prefer compressed files
  (MP3 or M4A at 128 to 192 kbps is about 1 MB a minute).
- **Names:** the file name is shown as the track's name; a leading track
  number is dropped (`01 - Clair_de_lune.mp3` becomes "Clair de lune").
- **Rights:** only add music you're allowed to use in an app you share.

Until this folder has a track, the app plays its calm sound loops (rain,
café, surf…), credited in `../CREDITS.md`.
