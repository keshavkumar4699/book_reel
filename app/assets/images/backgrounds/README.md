# Your background pictures

Put the pictures you want behind your reels in this folder, or straight in
`assets/images/` (any picture there except the `logo_*` files), then build
the app again. Each book opens on one of them at random, and while reading you
can tap the screen and pick another from the row at the top.

- **Formats:** JPG, PNG or WebP.
- **Size:** about 1080 × 2400 pixels (portrait) looks sharp on any phone and
  keeps the app small. Bigger pictures work, but every picture is packed
  into the app, so keep each one under about 1 MB.
- **Names:** the file name is shown as the picture's name
  (`lake_at_dawn.jpg` becomes "Lake at dawn").
- **Text stays readable:** the app measures each picture and shades it just
  enough for the text to keep its contrast, with light or dark text,
  whichever suits the picture.

Until this folder has a picture, the app uses its painted scenes.
